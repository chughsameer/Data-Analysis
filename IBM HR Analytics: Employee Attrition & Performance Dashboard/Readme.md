# 📊 IBM HR Analytics - Employee Attrition & Performance Dashboard

This Tableau dashboard analyzes employee attrition using the **IBM HR Analytics dataset**, providing insights into workforce trends and HR performance.

![Tableau Public - Book1 28-05-2025 22_14_28](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/221c74e1-68a7-4f9f-95bb-438bff72c117)


## 🚀 Objective
To explore the key factors influencing employee attrition and design an interactive dashboard for HR decision-making using Tableau.

## 📦 Dataset
- **Source**: IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition & Performance Dataset
- **Records**: 1470 employees  
- **Fields**: Age, Department, Education, Gender, Job Role, Monthly Income, Overtime, Job Satisfaction, and Attrition status

## 🧰 Tools Used
- Tableau (dashboard design & visual analytics)
- Microsoft Excel / CSV (data cleaning)

## 📌 Key Features
- **KPI Cards** for:
  - Attrition Rate
  - Total Attrition
  - Current Employees
- **Visual Insights**:
  - Overtime vs Attrition
  - Monthly Income vs Attrition
  - Departmental attrition (donut visualization)
  - Job Role analysis
  - Age group & gender-based attrition
  - Job Satisfaction vs Attrition

## 🔍 Key Insights
- High attrition among employees aged 25–35 and those working overtime
- Sales and R&D departments have the highest attrition rates
- Lower income and job satisfaction levels correlate with higher attrition
